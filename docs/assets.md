# UI 에셋

`assets/fonts/PretendardVariable.ttf`는 [Pretendard 공식 저장소](https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard)의 `packages/pretendard/dist/public/variable/PretendardVariable.ttf`를 사용한다. SIL Open Font License를 `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`에 포함했다.

`assets/images/eggs.png`와 `assets/images/strawberries.png`는 ImageGen 스킬의 기본 내장 도구로 2026-11-24 생성한 시연 상품 사진이다. 실제 판매 상품을 의미하지 않으며 실제 모집은 사용자가 선택한 사진을 표시한다. 최종 생성 프롬프트는 다음과 같다.

## Eggs

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: bundled demo product photograph for a Korean student grocery group-buying mobile app. Primary request: realistic high-quality editorial food product photo of a single open recycled pulp cardboard carton with ten clean brown eggs on a light warm ivory tabletop, subtly top-down three-quarter view. Soft natural daylight, warm neutral tones, tactile eggshells, entire carton comfortably within the square crop, simple uncluttered background. No hands, no people, no packaging text, no brand, no watermark. Deliver one square photograph.
```

## Strawberries

```text
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: bundled demo grocery product photo for a Korean student group-buying app. Primary request: realistic fresh red strawberries with tiny green leaves in a plain light recycled pulp produce tray resting on a warm ivory table. Soft natural daylight, top-down three-quarter view, whole tray within a square crop, appetizing natural fruit texture, simple uncluttered background, editorial catalog food photography. No hands, no people, no label, no lettering, no watermark. Deliver one square photograph.
```
